🎬 10 proven angles

Problem/solution, unboxing, testimonial template, comparison, claim-free before/after, FAQ, myth-bust, routine, gift and honest urgency. Every variant is a complete production pack, not just an idea.

🛡️ The guard is the feature

A deterministic banned-claim checker screens every script: no invented results, no fake discounts, no false scarcity, no medical or financial promises. Anything that trips it is withheld and reported.
